Pixel Walker: System Specification
Online Player Radar & Real-Time Map Presence
1. Overview & Core Vision
The Player Radar system is a cornerstone of the "Pixel Walker" social experience. By projecting active, online players onto a real-world 8-bit GPS map, the application transforms physical environments into a shared virtual ecosystem. This feature facilitates organic discovery, allowing players to view custom Paperdoll gear, engage in spontaneous party invites, and collaborate on localized Landmark Boss raids.
2. Technical Architecture & Spatial Indexing
Geohash Spatial Grid & Subscriptions
The system utilizes a Geohash precision model to manage network traffic and ensure relevant player density.


* Geohash Precision Level 7: The world is segmented into grids of approximately 150m x 150m. Client devices subscribe to WebSocket channels for their current cell and the eight surrounding adjacent cells (a 3x3 grid covering a ~450m radius).
* Update Frequency & Throttling:
   * Stationary / Pocket Walk Mode: Updates occur every 10–15 seconds or upon a 15-meter displacement.
   * Active Walk / Screen On Mode: High-frequency broadcasts every 3–5 seconds, utilizing client-side linear interpolation (Lerp) for smooth movement across the 8-bit tiles.
Security, Privacy & Safety Controls
Player safety is prioritized through automated masking and user-controlled visibility.


* Fuzzy Location Blur: To mitigate stalking risks, GPS coordinates are offset by a randomized 10–30 meter vector. This blur is removed only for players in the same party or on a mutual friends list.
* Ghost / Incognito Mode: A toggleable "Stealth Mode" hides the player's avatar from the public map. While active, the player is ineligible for proximity buffs or unsolicited party requests.
* Safe Zones: Private residences and sensitive locations can be masked globally, ensuring avatars only appear once they enter public thoroughfares.
3. Visual & Interactive Map Features
8-Bit Avatar Rendering & Paperdoll Display
The map provides high-fidelity visual representations of player progression and current activity.


* Real-Time Equipment Visibility: Current gear, fashion skins, and weapon sprites are visible to all nearby players.
* Title Auras: High-level achievements manifest as visual cues, such as the Pure VIT shield aura or Pure AGI shadow trails.
* Action Status Indicators: Floating 8-bit icons denote current player states:
   * ⚔️ In Combat: Engaged with a local mob or boss.
   * 🚶 Walking: Active movement in Pocket Walk mode.
   * 🛍️ Trading: Interacting with a Market or Outpost.
   * 💤 Idle / AFK: Stationary at a Landmark.
Proximity Interactions
Tapping a player sprite opens a contextual menu for immediate interaction:


1. Inspect Equipment: Detailed view of class, level, stats, and active Paperdoll layers.
2. Instant Party Invite: One-tap requests for nearby Landmark Dungeons (e.g., 7-Eleven Bosses).
3. Proximity Buff ("High-Five"): Moving within 10 meters of another player grants a +5% EXP or Movement Speed buff for 15 minutes.
4. Mercenary Beacon: Request to add the player as an asynchronous mercenary for future offline support.
